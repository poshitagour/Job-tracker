## Permit triage

The tracker flags jobs for manual review. It does not determine legal eligibility.

- **CSEP candidate:** technology/project/system duties may align with a Critical Skills occupation; verify SOC, duties, qualifications, remuneration and job duration.
- **GEP candidate:** potentially permit-eligible professional role; verify occupation, salary and LMNT requirements.
- **Avoid:** HR administrative occupation signal.
- **Unknown:** insufficient job information.
